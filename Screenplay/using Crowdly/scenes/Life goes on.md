# Life goes on

## Screenplay draft

EXT. LJUBLJANA - EARLY MORNING - 16 APRIL 1998

Veronika and Eduard sleep outside, close together. A POLICEMAN and POLICEWOMAN wake them.

POLICEMAN
You can go to the town hall to shelter yourself.

A QUICK ROLL of the night since their escape from Villette: the two of them getting away, moving through Ljubljana, and finally settling here to rest.

EDUARD
She... she's dead.

<!-- Context: Villette's prediction that Veronika would die the day after their escape was a lie. Eduard's line refers to that predicted death. Keep the dialogue as written. -->

VERONIKA
What's going on?

EDUARD
Nothing.

He helps Veronika to her feet.

EDUARD (CONT'D)
Or actually everything. You're alive. Tell me, how fucking amazing is that?

VERONIKA
Well, that sucks. What shall we do next?

EDUARD
(jokingly)
Let’s get married and have children.

FADE OUT.

TITLE: SOME TIME LATER

Veronika and Eduard marry. They live in a house on Eduard's parents' land.

Eduard's paintings begin to sell. His influential parents help make the introductions. Money comes in quickly. Eduard sets some aside, aware that sales may not last.

On the television: Matroskin the cat, sitting on a bench in *Three from Prostokvashino*, says, “I will save.”

Veronika begins to write.

## Continuity note

The timeline dates the Villette escape to 16 April 1998 and the marriage to 15–16 May 1998. The draft uses the novel chapter's “EARLY MORNING” for the opening scene; the existing screenplay note says “NIGHT.” Eduard's “She... she's dead” refers to Villette's false prediction that Veronika would die the day after their escape.
